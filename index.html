WHY THIS ARCHIVE EXISTS

Broadcasting is a team effort. Every career matters.

World Broadcast Biographies is an independent project documenting the people and professional journeys that shape broadcasting around the world. We aim to recognise experience, share knowledge and preserve stories across generations.

This is an independent archive and is not affiliated with any broadcaster, network or media organisation.
