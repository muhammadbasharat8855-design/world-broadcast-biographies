ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

Recognising the people who make broadcasting possible.

World Broadcast Biographies is an independent, international directory dedicated to documenting professional journeys and contributions across television, radio, sports and live production.

Our goal is to preserve accurate, respectful and well-sourced stories for students, industry colleagues and future generations.
